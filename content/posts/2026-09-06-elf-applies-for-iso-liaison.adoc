The EXPRESS Language Foundation has applied to establish a Category A
liaison with ISO/TC 184/SC 4, the ISO committee responsible for
industrial data and the ISO 10303 (STEP) series.

The working relationship between ELF and SC 4 already exists in practice:
the ISO 10303 STEP Resource Library published by SC 4 is built using ELF's
SUMA framework and ELF standards — Annotated EXPRESS (ELF 5001), the
EXPRESS changes model (ELF 5005), and the EXPRESS mapping language
(ELF 5006). ELF experts serve as convenors and project leaders across SC 4
working groups, and ELF continues to develop members of the EXPRESS
language family intended for standardization within SC 4, including
EXPRESS-Q and the EXPRESS mapping language.

A Category A liaison gives ELF's experts a single, official channel to
contribute this work directly to the committee's development of ISO 10303
and related International Standards. Short-term goals include introducing
ELF's work at the SC 4 plenary, participating in WG 11, WG 12, WG 21, and
JWG 24, and providing ELF's open-source EXPRESS toolchain for editing and
validating ISO 10303 schemas.

The application is available on the
link:https://www.expresslang.org/governance[Foundation's governance page]
together with its published directives and standards.
