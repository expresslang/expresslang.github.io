The EXPRESS Language Foundation is pleased to announce the launch of its
membership program.

ELF membership is open to all organizations and individuals worldwide that
support the Foundation's mission. Members participate and vote in the
Foundation's link:/technical-committees[technical committees] — one member,
one vote — and are represented in the Technical Coordination Committee
through committee chairs.

.Members founding in 2022 span six countries:

* Boeing, General Electric, NIST, PDES Inc., and STEP Tools (United States)
* Jotne EPM Technology (Norway)
* AFNeT Services (France)
* Ribose Limited (United Kingdom)
* Individual members from Austria, France, Japan, the United Kingdom, and the United States

Membership categories cover corporations of every size, small businesses,
non-profits and government bodies, universities, and individuals. The fee
schedule is a draft, pending approval by the Board of Directors ahead of
2027.

Organizations and individuals can apply through the
link:/join[membership application form]. The member roster is published at
link:/members[members].
