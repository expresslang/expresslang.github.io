== Manager, DI Strategy & Planning Department, Mitsubishi Heavy Industries

Yoshiaki Sonoda is a founding member of the EXPRESS Language Foundation. He is
the Manager of the DI Strategy & Planning Department at the Digital Innovation
Headquarters of Mitsubishi Heavy Industries, Ltd. (MHI), and the leader of the
company's International Standardization Strategy Task Force, which covers a
broad range of manufacturing industry domains, including power plants,
shipbuilding, aerospace, and defense.

== Current Roles

* *Manager, DI Strategy & Planning Department*, Digital Innovation

* *Leader, International Standardization Strategy Task Force*, Mitsubishi
  Heavy Industries (since 1989)
* *Convenor*, ISO/TC 184/SC 4/WG 25 — ISO CDD Validation Team (since 2025)
* *Deputy Convenor*, ISO/TC 184/SC 4/WG 3 — Oil, Gas, Process and Power

== Background

Yoshiaki specializes primarily in information technology supporting more than
700 products in MHI's manufacturing domains, and has been supporting their
revolutions in R&D activities for 35 years. He participates in numerous
committees at ISO and IEC as an expert.

Additionally, he has been involved in organizational culture reform for over
10 years, drawing on his knowledge of modern military organizational
strategies and East Asian philosophies — particularly Confucianism,
Shintoism, Buddhism, and Sun Tzu.
