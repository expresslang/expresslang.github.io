title: Membership
---

ELF membership is open to all organizations and individuals worldwide that
support the mission of the Foundation: the education, standardization,
research, promotion, definition, and usage of information modelling and
programming languages, with a focus on the EXPRESS language family.

.Members participate in the Foundation's technical life:

* participate and vote in any link:/technical-committees[Technical Committee] —
one member, one vote;
* propose new technical committees and chair their work;
* be represented in the Technical Coordination Committee through committee
chairs;
* access work in progress, drafts, and member discussions;
* be listed on the link:/members[Members] page.

Corporate governance — budgets, legal matters, and bylaws — remains the
responsibility of the link:/governance[Board of Directors].

All members share the same participation rights regardless of category:
each member organization appoints one delegate, and each individual member
participates directly. Voting in technical committees is one member, one
vote — never proportional to size. Dues fund the Foundation's exempt
purposes; they are not deductible as charitable contributions where
membership benefits are received.

== How to join

. Choose the category matching your organization or status.
. Write to link:mailto:info@expresslang.org[info@expresslang.org] to
request the membership agreement.
. Return the signed agreement — the Board reviews and approves membership
per the bylaws.
. Upon approval you receive an invoice; once dues are settled, your
membership is activated and listed on the link:/members[Members] page.

Founding membership is by Board invitation and carries the same rights as
corporate membership without the requirement of an annual donation.
