**Subject:** Re: EXPRESS Language Foundation — Category A liaison application assessment

Dear [Reviewer name],

Thank you for your careful assessment of our Category A liaison application, and for the constructive way in which the findings on criteria 3 and 5 were presented. They were fair, and we have treated them as a work list.

Following your assessment, the Foundation has made the following changes, all now published on our website:

- **A categorized membership roster.** The former "supporters" page has been retired and replaced by a members directory (https://www.expresslang.org/members) listing eight founding organizations and twelve individual members, each with a country attribution. Our members are represented across six countries — the United States, France, Norway, Japan, the United Kingdom, and Austria — and the program is open to applicants worldwide.
- **A fee-based membership program.** Categories and annual fees, banded by organization revenue and size (US$550–US$12,000), are published at https://www.expresslang.org/membership, together with the rights each category carries. Membership is open to organizations and individuals globally, and the fee schedule is pending adoption by resolution of our Board of Directors.
- **A documented consensus process.** Our governance page (https://www.expresslang.org/governance) now describes the three-layer model in which technical authority is delegated from the Board to a Technical Coordination Committee and five technical committees; decisions are made by consensus, with a supermajority fallback on a one-member-one-vote basis, and a defined escalation path for formal objections. Public review under our standardization directives, published comment dispositions, and open comment channels for non-members complete the picture.

We would be glad to provide an updated liaison form reflecting criteria 3 and 5, together with the adopted Board resolution once it is in force, if that would assist the committee's re-assessment.

Thank you again for your time and guidance. We remain committed to open, worldwide membership and to consensus-based standardization of the EXPRESS language family, and we welcome any further advice the committee may have.
