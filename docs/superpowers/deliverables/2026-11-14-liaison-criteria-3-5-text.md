# Liaison Form Replacement Text — ISO/TC 184/SC 4 Criteria 3 and 5

**Document date:** 2026-11-14
**Purpose:** Replacement text for the "Details / relevant clause in statutes" fields of the Foundation's Category A liaison application to ISO/TC 184/SC 4, responding to the reviewer's assessment of criterion 3 (membership-based, open worldwide) and criterion 5 (stakeholder engagement and consensus decision-making). Each field is a single concise paragraph, ready to paste into the form.

---

## Criterion 3 — "is membership-based and open to members worldwide or over a broad region"

> The EXPRESS Language Foundation is membership-based and open worldwide to both organizations and natural persons. Its bylaws provide the statutory basis: "Membership shall be open to individuals and organizations involved or interested in the mission of the Corporation, subject to Board approval" (Bylaws, Section 3.01, https://www.expresslang.org/documents/elf-bylaws.pdf). A categorized, fee-based membership program is published at https://www.expresslang.org/membership, with annual dues banded by organization revenue and size (US$550 to US$12,000) and an individual category at US$550; Founding membership is by Board invitation. The current membership roster — eight founding organizations and twelve individual members — is published at https://www.expresslang.org/members, and its members are represented across six countries: the United States, France, Norway, Japan, the United Kingdom, and Austria. Membership remains open to applicants worldwide; admission is by signed membership agreement approved by the Board under Section 3.01 of the bylaws.

## Criterion 5 — "has a process for stakeholder engagement and consensus decision-making"

> Stakeholder engagement and consensus decision-making are documented in the Foundation's published governance model at https://www.expresslang.org/governance. Technical work is carried out in five technical committees, coordinated by a Technical Coordination Committee composed of the chairs of the active committees and a Board representative, under the Board of Directors, which retains corporate governance and final appeal authority. Decisions in the committees are made by consensus of participating members; where consensus cannot be reached, a supermajority vote settles the question — one member, one vote. Formal objections escalate from the committee to the Technical Coordination Committee and, finally, to the Board. All drafts are publicly reviewed in accordance with the ELF standardization directives (Directive 101, https://www.expresslang.org/docs/documents/directive-standardization/document.html), every comment receives a published disposition, and non-members may comment on all work through open channels. The governing clauses are the Foundation's bylaws (https://www.expresslang.org/documents/elf-bylaws.pdf, in particular Articles III, IV, and XI) and the resolutions of the Board of Directors that delegate technical authority to the technical committee system and define member participation and voting within it (2026), which will be published with the governance documents at https://www.expresslang.org/governance.
