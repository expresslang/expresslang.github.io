# Board Resolution Outline — Membership Program and Delegation of Technical Authority

**Express Language Foundation**
A Delaware non-profit public benefit corporation, recognized as tax-exempt under Section 501(c)(3) of the United States Internal Revenue Code (bylaws §2.03(a))

**Document date:** 2026-11-14
**Status:** Draft for adoption by the Board of Directors
**Prepared in connection with:** the Foundation's Category A liaison application to ISO/TC 184/SC 4, and the membership program published at https://www.expresslang.org/membership

---

## Purpose

This document sets out, in full, the four resolutions the Board of Directors is asked to adopt in order to place the Foundation's membership program and its technical governance on a formal corporate footing. The Foundation's website already publishes the substance of each resolution — the membership categories and fees, the members roster, the governance model, and the technical committee structure — and the published pages carry a note that the fee schedule is subject to adoption by Board resolution. Adoption of these resolutions makes the published material authoritative and completes the evidence for criteria 3 and 5 of the ISO/TC 184/SC 4 liaison eligibility assessment: that the Foundation is membership-based and open to members worldwide, and that it has a documented process for stakeholder engagement and consensus decision-making.

The resolutions are drafted so that they operate entirely within the existing bylaws. No bylaw amendment is required. Each operative clause below cites the bylaw provision that authorizes it.

## Recitals

**WHEREAS**, the EXPRESS Language Foundation is a Delaware non-profit public benefit corporation organized exclusively for charitable, religious, educational, and scientific purposes, and recognized as tax exempt under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code (bylaws §2.03(a));

**WHEREAS**, the purposes of the Foundation include the education, standardization, research, promotion, definition, and usage of information modelling and programming languages, achieved in part by creating international standards (bylaws §2.01);

**WHEREAS**, all corporate powers shall be exercised by or under the authority of the Board of Directors, and the affairs of the Foundation shall be managed under the direction of the Board (bylaws §4.02);

**WHEREAS**, membership shall be open to individuals and organizations involved or interested in the mission of the Corporation, subject to Board approval (bylaws §3.01);

**WHEREAS**, except as determined by a majority vote of the Board of Directors, membership shall be a due-paying, non-voting membership, and dues shall be determined by the Board of Directors annually (bylaws §3.02);

**WHEREAS**, the Foundation has applied for Category A liaison with ISO/TC 184/SC 4, the eligibility criteria of which include that a liaison organization is membership-based and open to members worldwide or over a broad region (criterion 3) and has a process for stakeholder engagement and consensus decision-making (criterion 5);

**WHEREAS**, the Foundation has published a categorized, fee-based membership program open worldwide to organizations and individuals, a public members roster, a three-layer governance model separating corporate governance from technical governance, and five technical committees whose work proceeds by public review and consensus, together with the standardization directives that govern that review (ELF Directive 101);

**WHEREAS**, the Board finds that admitting members, delegating technical authority to the members-directed technical committee system, and defining member participation in that system advance the Foundation's exempt purposes and strengthen the openness, worldwide character, and consensus discipline of its standards work;

**NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED**, that the Board of Directors adopts the following four resolutions.

## Resolution 1 — Delegation of technical authority to the technical committee system

The Board of Directors hereby delegates to the Foundation's technical governance system — comprising the technical committees and the Technical Coordination Committee — the authority to direct and approve the development of the Foundation's technical work products, including the specifications, directives, schemas, vocabularies, and educational materials of the EXPRESS language family and its related initiatives. The delegation is subject to the following terms:

The Technical Coordination Committee (the "TCC") is composed of the chairs of the active technical committees and one representative designated by the Board. This resolution defines the TCC by role and designates no individual to any office; the chairs of the technical committees are determined within each committee under procedures the TCC maintains. The TCC meets regularly, coordinates the work of the technical committees, approves the formation of new technical committees, adopts and amends their charters, and gives final approval for the publication of committee documents after Last Call.

The technical committees conduct their work in the open: all drafts are publicly reviewed in accordance with the ELF standardization directives (Directive 101), every comment receives a published disposition, and committee decisions are made as provided in Resolution 2.

This delegation concerns technical authority only. All corporate powers — budget, legal matters, bylaws, and final appeals — remain with the Board, which may modify, suspend, or revoke this delegation, or reserve any category of decision to itself, at any time by majority vote. Nothing in this resolution designates a committee of the Board under Article V of the bylaws or delegates any power that bylaws §5.01 reserves to the Board; the TCC acts under delegated technical authority as an element of the management of the Foundation's affairs under the direction of the Board (bylaws §4.02), and the Board retains final appeal authority over all decisions taken under this delegation.

## Resolution 2 — Member participation and voting in technical committees

The Board first finds and declares the following, so that the scope of this resolution is unambiguous. The membership established under bylaws §3.01 and §3.02 is, and remains, a due-paying, non-voting membership with respect to all corporate governance: members have no voting rights in the election of Directors, the amendment of bylaws, the adoption of budgets, or any other matter reserved to the Board, consistent with the Foundation's obligations as a Section 501(c)(3) organization. This resolution does not alter that. It exercises the express exception in bylaws §3.02 — "Except as determined by a majority vote of the Board of Directors" — to determine that member voting applies within the Foundation's technical committee system only, and it may be modified or withdrawn by the Board at any time by majority vote.

Subject to the foregoing, the Board resolves as follows. Every member in good standing may participate and vote in any technical committee of the Foundation, regardless of membership category or fee level. Each member organization appoints one natural person as its delegate, and that delegate casts the organization's vote; each individual member participates and votes personally. Voting in technical committees is one member, one vote, and is never proportional to an organization's size, revenue, employee count, or contribution level. Members may propose the formation of new technical committees and may chair them, consistent with Resolution 1 and the TCC's charter-approval authority.

Committee decisions are made by consensus of the participating members. Where consensus cannot be reached after genuine effort, the question is settled by a supermajority vote of the participating members on the one-member-one-vote basis stated above. A member may enter a formal objection to a decision; such objections are considered first by the committee, then by the TCC, and ultimately by the Board, whose decision is final. The Board directs that these procedures — consensus effort, supermajority fallback, and objection escalation from committee to TCC to Board — be documented and published so that members and the public can rely on them.

## Resolution 3 — Adoption of the membership fee schedule

Pursuant to bylaws §3.02, which provides that dues shall be determined by the Board of Directors annually, the Board adopts the following schedule of annual membership fees, which corresponds to the schedule published at https://www.expresslang.org/membership. Founding membership is by Board invitation and carries the same participation rights as corporate membership without the requirement of an annual donation.

| Category | Annual fee | Eligibility |
|---|---|---|
| Founding | By Board invitation | Inaugural members designated by the Board |
| Corporate — revenue < $10M | $3,000 | For-profit organizations |
| Corporate — revenue $10–100M | $6,000 | For-profit organizations |
| Corporate — revenue > $100M | $12,000 | For-profit organizations |
| Small business | $2,000 | For-profit, ≤50 employees (self-certified; threshold subject to Board adoption like the fees) |
| Non-profit / Government | $1,200 | Registered non-profits, government bodies |
| University | $550 | Accredited academic institutions |
| Individual | $550 | Natural persons |

The schedule takes effect upon adoption of this resolution. The Board shall review the schedule at least annually and may amend it by resolution; dues for any subsequent year are determined by the Board annually as provided in bylaws §3.02. Small-business eligibility is self-certified by the applicant. The Board notes that membership dues fund the Foundation's exempt purposes and are not charitable-contribution deductions where membership benefits are received, consistent with the notice published on the membership page. Upon adoption, the note on the membership page stating that the fee schedule is subject to adoption by Board resolution may be updated to record the adoption.

## Drafting notes (not part of the resolutions)

- The resolutions deliberately name no chairs of technical committees. The TCC is defined wholly by role — "the chairs of the active technical committees" — and chairs are determined within each committee under procedures the TCC maintains. When the Board designates its TCC representative, that designation should be recorded as a separate, short resolution or minute entry, not by amending these texts.
- Resolution 2 recommends, but does not fix, the numeric supermajority threshold; the TCC should fix it (a common default is two-thirds of the votes cast) in the published consensus procedures so that the fallback is operational before it is first needed.
- The recitals quote the bylaws verbatim: §3.01 ("Membership shall be open to individuals and organizations involved or interested in the mission of the Corporation, subject to Board approval."), §3.02 ("Except as determined by a majority vote of the Board of Directors, membership shall be a due-paying, non-voting membership. Dues shall be determined by the Board of Directors annually."), §4.02, §5.01, and §11.05. If the bylaws are amended, re-verify these quotations before adoption.
- The fee table in Resolution 3 is the schedule published on the membership page (https://www.expresslang.org/membership), which currently carries the note "The fee schedule is subject to adoption by resolution of the Board of Directors." That note can be replaced once this resolution passes.
